# Safety and privacy

- The prototype must clearly state that mock mode is not image recognition.
- Never claim a path, crossing, or movement is safe.
- Never invent distances or imply complete hazard detection.
- Keep descriptions concise, factual, and uncertainty-aware.
- Camera images and audio should not be retained by default.
- Provider keys must remain server-side if live AI is added in a future approved milestone.
- SOS requires deliberate confirmation and leaves the final call action to the user.
- This tool does not replace a white cane, guide dog, mobility training, or human assistance.
