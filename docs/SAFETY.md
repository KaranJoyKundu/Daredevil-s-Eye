# Safety and privacy

- Experimental assistive-vision prototype; not a certified medical device, navigation aid or emergency-dispatch system.
- Never claim a path, crossing or movement is safe.
- Never invent distances, directions or measurements.
- Keep descriptions concise, factual and uncertainty-aware.
- Do not identify people or infer sensitive personal traits.
- Camera images and microphone audio are not intentionally persisted by application code.
- Camera images are forwarded to Gemini for analysis. If Gemini TTS is enabled, response text is sent to Google's speech service.
- Browser speech recognition may send audio to the browser vendor's recognition service.
- Provider keys and owner app tokens must remain server-side.
- SOS requires deliberate sender action. Receiver must be paired, open, online and able to play audio.
- SOS is not fall detection and cannot replace emergency services, a white cane, guide dog, mobility training or human assistance.
