Before using: camera photos and microphone recordings are sent to the app backend and Google Gemini for analysis/transcription. The app compresses phone photos to JPEG below 3.8 MB before sending. Photos and answers are not saved to an app account unless you sign in and enable the optional history switch in Admin. When enabled, the compressed photo, prompt, answer and usage details are saved privately to your account; the saved photo record includes a keyed IP fingerprint (not the raw IP) until you delete it; usage-only fingerprints expire after 90 days. Saved history can be reviewed and deleted from Account & History. Voice recordings are processed for transcription but are not intentionally stored by this app. Do not submit sensitive information or rely on AI descriptions as a guarantee of safety.
