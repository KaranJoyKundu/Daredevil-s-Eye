Help and instructions

Describe

Press DESCRIBE to capture one photo and hear a short AI description.

Ask AI

Press ASK AI and speak a question, or type one on the home screen. The current prototype captures a photo to provide visual context.

SOS

Configure a trusted contact in Admin / Settings. SOS asks for confirmation before opening your phone dialler; it does not silently place a call.

Camera connection

Your phone and ESP32-CAM must be on a network that allows the phone browser to reach the camera's /capture endpoint. Set the camera base address in Admin / Settings.

Important safety note

This is an experimental assistant. AI may miss obstacles, misread scenes, or be wrong. Never rely on it to decide whether a path or road crossing is safe. Continue using your cane, guide dog, and mobility skills.
