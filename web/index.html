Alerts family with SOS

Tap SOS twice within five seconds and a paired phone gets a push alert. It does not call emergency services.

Routes each request through one Worker

A Cloudflare Worker checks the photo, picks a Gemini model and tries another if one fails.

Why Bangla first

Most camera assistants answer in English. Daredevil’s Eye answers in Bangla, understands Bangla and English mixed in one sentence, and leaves every decision with the person holding the phone.

The AI assistant reports, you decide

Built as a Human+AI copilot: the person holding the phone makes every decision.

Copilot: Gemini vision with spoken Bangla answers.

Memory: a private history of photos and answers.

Orchestration: one Worker for models, speech and SOS.

Questions

Short answers about safety, data and phones.

Read the full terms

Does the assistant replace a cane or a guide dog?

No. It is an experimental helper. Keep using your cane, guide dog and the people you trust.

Does it answer in Bangla and English?

Bangla or English, switched with one tap. You can speak Bangla, English or a mix.

Does it need an install on Android?

No. It runs in Chrome on Android, and you can add it to the home screen.

Where do photos go after Describe?

To the Cloudflare Worker and, in Gemini mode, Google Gemini for analysis. Photo history is currently off by default; new submissions are not saved unless the client explicitly enables history. Do not send confidential images.

Does SOS call 999?

No. It sends a push alert to one paired phone, and a sent push is not proof of delivery.

Which camera does Describe use?

The phone camera, or an ESP32-CAM on the same Wi-Fi. It takes up to three frames and keeps the sharpest.
