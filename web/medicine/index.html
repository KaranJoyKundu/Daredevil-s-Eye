Medicine, spoken aloud.

Pair your ESP32 + RC522 reader with this Daredevil’s Eye account. When a registered medicine card is scanned, the name is delivered to your phone and spoken using your selected app language — even while you're browsing another Daredevil’s Eye page.

The phone must be signed in, online, and have the RFID listener enabled. Keep Daredevil’s Eye open for the first version; background and screen-locked delivery needs separate Android testing.

1 · Pair your reader

Generate a one-time code while signed in. Enter it into the ESP32 serial monitor when the firmware asks for it. The code expires after 10 minutes.

Hardware setup

The firmware will use your existing RC522 wiring and UID-to-medicine mapping. Wi-Fi credentials and the device token are entered/stored on the ESP32, never in this page's source code.

RC522  →  ESP32
SDA/SS →  GPIO 5
SCK    →  GPIO 18
MOSI   →  GPIO 23
MISO   →  GPIO 19
RST    →  GPIO 22
3.3V   →  3V3
GND    →  GND

Use 3.3V only. The reader's SDA pin is SPI chip-select, not I²C SDA.
