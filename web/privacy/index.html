Privacy

Camera images

When you use Describe or Ask AI, a still image from the selected source (ESP32-CAM, phone photo or uploaded file) is sent to this app's backend. In Gemini mode, the backend forwards the image to Google Gemini for analysis. Do not send images unless you are comfortable sharing their contents with Google.

Microphone and speech recognition

Microphone access is requested only after you tap a microphone action. The app does not intentionally record or store audio. Browser speech recognition is provided by the browser and may send audio to the browser vendor's recognition service. Its processing and retention are governed by that provider's terms and settings.

Spoken responses

If Gemini TTS is selected, the generated response text is sent to Google's Gemini speech service. Device speech synthesis uses voices made available by your operating system or browser.

Storage

Camera images and AI responses are not intentionally saved on this app's server. Device preferences and a per-install SOS sender identifier are stored in this browser's local storage. The paired SOS receiver token is stored in session storage for the current browser tab. A versioned privacy-consent record is stored in local storage.

Emergency alerts

SOS invitation codes are single-use and expire after ten minutes. A paired receiver token is stored in session storage and can be revoked by generating a new invitation. The relay stores the active alert status and timestamp, not camera images or microphone recordings.

API key and free-tier processing

The Gemini API key remains in the Worker environment and is never placed in browser source files or committed to the repository. This public demo uses Google's Gemini free tier where available. Google states that free-tier API content may be used to improve its products. Do not upload sensitive or confidential images unless you are comfortable with Google's free-tier data-use terms.

Consent

You can reset your consent below. You will be asked to review the current notice again before using the camera.

Prototype limitations

This is not a production privacy or safety certification. AI may miss hazards. The SOS receiver cannot guarantee delivery if the receiving device is offline, locked, suspended or has closed its browser.
