DATA & PERMISSIONS

Privacy

What the app processes, what it stores, which services receive it and how you can remove saved data.

Camera images and AI analysis

When you use Describe, Ask AI, the phone camera or Upload photo, the selected image is processed in your browser and sent to this app's Cloudflare Worker backend. Phone photos and uploads are resized and re-encoded as JPEG below 3.8 MB before they are sent. In Gemini mode, the Worker forwards the image and your question or prompt to Google Gemini. Google processes that content under the terms for the Gemini service and API key being used. Do not submit images unless you are comfortable sharing their contents with Google.

Optional account photo and answer history

After you accept the Terms & Conditions, photo history is enabled by default. Each compressed JPEG submitted for AI analysis is saved to the private bucket before Gemini runs, so images remain recorded even when an AI request fails. Previewed photos that are cancelled before submission are not saved. Signed-in users see their photos, questions, prompts, answers, model details and token usage in Account & History; those records remain until they delete them. Signed-out visitors get private guest history tied to a secure HttpOnly browser cookie. Guest photos/records are automatically deleted after 30 days. Clearing site data can remove access to guest history sooner. You can turn off new photo saving in Admin at any time. The bucket is not public and the account page uses short-lived viewing links.

History is kept until you delete it. You can delete one saved photo/answer at a time or delete all saved history and usage from your Account page. Turning the option off stops new photo and answer records from being saved; it does not automatically delete older records.

Usage records and IP information

Signed-in usage rows can record model, request type, token counts, photo size, timestamp and a keyed pseudonymous IP fingerprint. Guest photo records can also include a keyed HMAC IP fingerprint for aggregate counts. Raw IP addresses are never stored, and guest browser user-agent strings are not stored. A guest fingerprint is deleted with its photo record after 30 days or sooner if you delete it. Signed-in photo fingerprints remain with their record until you delete it; separate usage-only rows expire after 90 days and can also be deleted from your Account page.

Microphone and voice transcription

Microphone permission is requested only after you tap a voice action. Automatic mode records a short audio clip in the browser and sends it to Gemini for transcription. When Bangla mode is selected, transcription is requested in Bengali script; English mode requests an English transcript. The app does not intentionally save the audio recording or transcript in its photo-history database. If account usage saving is enabled, model and token-usage metadata may be recorded, but not the audio bytes. Browser-first speech recognition uses the browser's recognition service, which may process audio through the browser vendor under its own terms. Do not speak passwords, private details or confidential information.

Your Gemini API keys

A personal Gemini key entered directly in Admin is kept only in that browser tab's session storage and is removed when the tab closes. Signed-in users can instead save a personal Gemini key to their account. That saved key is encrypted by the Worker using AES-256-GCM before it is stored in the database. The plaintext key is not returned to the browser or stored in plaintext; the Worker decrypts it in memory only when making a Gemini request. You can replace or delete the saved key from Account & History. The shared project key, when configured, is stored as a Cloudflare Worker secret.

Account service and current availability

The Supabase project, private photo bucket and account/guest history schema have been provisioned. Persistence becomes active on the live site when the Cloudflare Worker receives the Supabase URL, publishable key, Secret key and encryption secrets. Until the Account page reports that the history service is ready, do not assume a photo was saved. Personal Gemini keys are encrypted by the Worker before database storage.

Device storage

Camera address, speech preferences, consent state, emergency contact details and a per-install SOS sender identifier are saved in this browser's local storage. The paired SOS receiver token is stored in local storage on the receiver device so it can reconnect after the tab closes. A browser push subscription (endpoint and cryptographic subscription keys) is held in the sender's Cloudflare Durable Object so screen-off Web Push notifications can be sent. Re-pairing replaces the old receiver subscription.

Emergency alerts and notifications

The SOS relay stores the active alert and its timestamps for up to ten minutes. Push notifications are sent through the browser's push service and contain only a generic alert message; the receiver retrieves the alert details over its paired session. Push acceptance is not proof that the other phone displayed the notification. Delivery may be delayed or blocked by offline devices, notification settings, Do Not Disturb, browser support or Android battery policy. Chrome on Android is recommended for this VAPID push implementation. SOS does not call emergency services.

Shake and voice SOS

Shake-to-SOS is optional and disabled by default. When enabled, accelerometer readings are evaluated locally while Blind mode is visible and are not sent to the Worker. Voice SOS is optional and disabled by default. It uses browser speech recognition only while Blind mode is open and the page is visible; the browser's recognition provider may process audio outside this app. No SOS audio recording is intentionally stored by Daredevil's Eye.

Spoken responses and assistive limitations

Device speech synthesis uses voices supplied by Android or the browser. Bangla mode requests the bn-BD locale; Chrome may not expose every Android voice in its voice list, so use the Admin voice preview to check actual playback. Daredevil's Eye is an experimental project, not a certified navigation aid, medical device or emergency-dispatch service. AI can miss hazards; continue using a cane, guide dog, mobility training and human assistance.

External services and free-tier use

Images and voice recordings submitted for AI analysis/transcription are processed by Google Gemini. Account authentication, database records and private photo objects use Supabase once configured. SOS push notifications are handled by the browser's push provider. Each provider has its own privacy terms and service availability. Google may treat free-tier and paid-tier content differently; review the current terms for the key you use and avoid sending confidential images or audio through a shared free-tier key.

Reset consent

You can reset the privacy-consent record on this device below. The notice will be shown again before camera use. Resetting local consent does not delete account history; use the Account page to delete saved photos, answers and usage.

Prototype status

This is not a production privacy or safety certification. Account history is opt-in and requires server configuration. Account deletion is not yet self-service; you can delete saved photos/answers, usage and personal Gemini keys from your Account page, and contact the project owner for account removal.
