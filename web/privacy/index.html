DATA & PERMISSIONS

Privacy

What the prototype sends, what it stores and what stays on your device.

Camera images and AI analysis

When you use Describe, Ask AI or the image tester, a still image from the selected source (ESP32-CAM, phone camera or uploaded file) is sent to this app's Worker backend. In Gemini mode, the Worker forwards the image to Google Gemini. Do not submit images unless you are comfortable sharing their contents with Google.

Microphone and automatic voice fallback

Microphone permission is requested only after you tap a voice action. The app first attempts the browser's native speech-recognition service, which may process audio through the browser vendor under its own terms. If that service is unavailable or reports a network/service error, the app can record a short audio clip in the browser and send it to the Gemini API for transcription and language detection. Bengali words are translated to English for the question; English speech is preserved even when spoken with a Bangladeshi accent.

The app does not intentionally keep audio recordings or transcripts in its server database. The audio is sent to Google for transcription, so Google's processing and retention terms apply. Do not speak passwords, private details or confidential information.

Spoken responses

Device speech synthesis uses voices supplied by your operating system or browser. The current Gemini TTS selection in Admin is a legacy option and may not be available on the deployed Worker; device speech is the recommended option for this prototype.

Storage on this device

Camera images and AI responses are not intentionally saved on this app's server. Device preferences, camera address, emergency contact and a per-install SOS sender identifier are saved in this browser's local storage. The paired SOS receiver token is stored in session storage for the current browser tab. A versioned privacy-consent record is also stored in local storage.

Your own Gemini API key (BYOK)

If you enter a personal Gemini API key in Admin, it is held in this tab's session storage and sent in a request header only to this site's API routes that need a provider key. It is not added to the app's local preferences, database, repository or Worker secret. Closing the tab clears the session key. You can remove it immediately from Admin. The key is sent to the Worker and used to authenticate with Google; use only a key you own and are comfortable using here.

Emergency alerts

SOS invitation codes are single-use and expire after ten minutes. A paired receiver token is stored in session storage and can be revoked by generating a new invitation. The relay stores active alert status and timestamps, not camera images or microphone recordings. Delivery is not guaranteed when a phone is offline, locked or suspended.

Gemini free-tier data use and quotas

The public demo uses the configured Gemini project key when you have not supplied your own key. Google states that content submitted through its free tier may be used to improve its products. Do not upload sensitive or confidential images unless you are comfortable with Google's current free-tier terms. Google also enforces model and project-level request/token quotas. Automatic model fallback cannot bypass a project-wide limit.

Account system status

This prototype does not currently have user accounts, email verification, per-user usage history or an administrator usage database. Those are planned future features, not implemented collection activities. The app therefore does not currently collect a visitor name, signup phone number or signup email.

Consent

You can reset your consent below. You will be asked to review the current notice again before using the camera.

Prototype limitations

This is not a production privacy or safety certification. AI may miss hazards. SOS does not replace emergency services. Before adding accounts or IP-based quotas, the project needs a documented retention policy, data-access controls, deletion process and applicable privacy review.
