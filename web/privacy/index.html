Privacy

Camera images

When you use Describe or Ask AI, the browser captures an image from the configured ESP32-CAM and sends it to this app's backend. In Gemini mode, the backend forwards that image to Google Gemini for analysis. Do not capture or send images unless you are comfortable sharing their contents with the configured provider.

Storage

This prototype does not intentionally save camera images or AI responses on its server. Device settings, including your camera address, trusted contact, and optional prompt, are stored in this browser's local storage.

API key

The Gemini API key belongs only in the local Worker environment file. It must never be entered into this page or committed to the repository.

Prototype limitations

This is not a production privacy or security certification. Do not use it with sensitive images or information.
