SOS & Triggers

Pair the sender and receiver in the SOS Center. Configure only the optional trigger preferences here.

Optional hands-free SOS triggers

These are disabled by default. They work only while Blind mode is open and visible on a supported phone. Shake sensing stays on the device; voice wake-word recognition may send audio to the browser's speech provider. Each automatic trigger has a cancellation window.

Disabled. Three strong, separate shakes within a short window are required.

Voice SOS uses the browser's continuous speech-recognition API, not Gemini. The microphone is monitored only in the visible Blind mode page. English: say “Daredevil SOS”, then “Confirm SOS”. Bangla: say “ডেয়ারডেভিল এসওএস”, then “নিশ্চিত করুন” or “এসওএস পাঠান”. Say “Cancel SOS” / “এসওএস বাতিল” during the countdown to stop it. Recognition availability depends on Android/Chrome and installed speech services.

Voice & Accessibility

Automatic mode records a short clip and sends it to Gemini to detect Bangla, English or mixed speech. Browser mode uses the selected language first and switches to Gemini if it fails. Audio is processed externally; do not speak confidential information.

Gemini offers 14 prebuilt female voices, along with other voices. Gemini TTS is not wired to this Worker yet. Use device voices for the live demo; Gemini TTS can be added in a later phase.

Voice options depend on the voices installed in your browser or operating system.

These styles adjust the selected device voice. They do not guarantee a specific gender or sound; available voices vary by device. You can select a named installed voice above.
