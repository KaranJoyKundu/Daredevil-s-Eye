Android volume shortcuts

These controls are available only inside the installed Daredevil's Eye Android app. The global shortcuts require you to enable the Accessibility Service yourself in Android Settings.

Open Accessibility settingsFind Daredevil's Eye under Installed apps / Downloaded apps and enable its service.
Check Accessibility ServiceCheck whether Android currently reports the global shortcut service as enabled.
Foreground volume shortcutsUse the approved controls while Daredevil's Eye is open. Turn off to restore ordinary volume adjustment in the foreground.
Global volume shortcutsAllow shortcuts while other apps are open, when the Accessibility Service is enabled. Android restrictions and TalkBack conflicts still apply.

Approved button mapping

Volume UpDescribe the current scene.
Volume DownAsk AI using the selected image and microphone.
Double Volume UpRepeat the last answer; must not also trigger Describe.
Hold both volume buttonsOpen SOS confirmation. Holding the keys alone must never dispatch an alert.

Important Android limitation: Screen-off, lock-screen, background camera/microphone and global key behaviour are not guaranteed. Accessibility Service use is explicit and may conflict with TalkBack. Test each state on the target handset before relying on it.
