Terms & Conditions

What using Daredevil's Eye means, how information is handled, and what the service can and cannot do.

1. Agreement and privacy notice

By using Daredevil's Eye, you agree to these Terms & Conditions and the Privacy notice. The privacy notice is informational and remains visible at the top of the app. There is no blocking accept/reject popup, no required checkbox and no consent button. The app remains usable; using an AI feature still sends the selected content to the services described in the Privacy page.

2. What the service does

Daredevil's Eye can capture or accept a photo, send it for AI analysis, show and speak a response, transcribe a short voice question, and relay an SOS alert to a paired receiver. Available features depend on your browser, network, device permissions and third-party services.

3. Important safety limits

This is an experimental project. It is not a certified mobility or navigation device, medical device, fall detector, emergency-dispatch service or substitute for human assistance. AI may misunderstand an image, omit an obstacle, give an incorrect description or fail to respond. Never rely on it alone to cross a road, navigate stairs, judge whether an area is safe, take medication or make an emergency decision. Continue using your white cane, guide dog, mobility training and established emergency plan. In an emergency, contact local emergency services or your trusted contact directly.

4. Photos, prompts and voice

When you take or upload a photo, the browser may resize and compress it, then sends it to this app's Cloudflare Worker and, when Gemini is selected, Google Gemini for analysis. Your typed question and app prompt are sent with the image. When you use voice input, a short audio recording may be sent to Gemini for transcription. Browser speech recognition may use the browser/device provider. Do not submit confidential images, passwords, financial documents or private conversations.

5. Optional photo history

Photo history is optional and currently off by default in the client. A photo is saved only when the request explicitly enables history and the Worker has its Supabase history service configured. The Admin photo-history toggle has been removed, and no user-facing opt-in control is currently exposed; do not assume new submissions are being saved. A photo preview that is cancelled before submission is not sent for AI analysis and is not saved.

When records already exist, signed-in users can view and delete their account history in Account & History. Signed-out guest records are tied to a secure browser cookie and, when created, are automatically deleted after 30 days. Clearing site data may remove access to guest history sooner. The private storage bucket is not publicly accessible; viewing links are short-lived.

Usage records may contain model, request type, token counts, photo size, timestamps and a keyed pseudonymous IP fingerprint. Raw IP addresses are not stored. Guest records and their fingerprints are deleted with the photo record after 30 days or sooner if you delete them. Signed-in records remain until deleted; separate usage-only records expire after 90 days.

You can delete individual saved photos or all existing account/guest history from Account & History. Account deletion is not yet self-service; contact the project owner for account removal.

6. Accounts and personal Gemini API keys

You are responsible for keeping your account password and any personal Gemini API key private. If you save a Gemini key to your account, the Worker encrypts it before database storage. Do not share your login credentials or enter another person's API key without permission. The AI service may apply quotas, rate limits and its own terms; using your own key does not guarantee availability.

7. SOS and notifications

SOS alerts are best-effort messages to a paired receiver. The receiving phone must have a valid pairing and working internet/notification setup. A push service accepting a request does not prove that the notification was shown, heard or read. Alerts can be delayed, blocked by device settings or missed while a phone is offline or restricted by battery policy. The app does not call emergency services and does not guarantee SOS delivery.

8. Third-party services

The service may rely on Google Gemini for AI, Cloudflare for hosting/Worker processing and push relay, Supabase for account authentication/database/private photo storage, and browser/operating-system providers for speech and notifications. Those providers operate under their own terms, privacy policies, quotas and availability. Your use of a particular service may be subject to the policies shown by that provider.

9. Responsible use

Use the service lawfully, only with permission to share the images and audio you submit, and without attempting to interfere with its infrastructure or another person's account. You are responsible for checking AI output and recipient details before acting on it or sending money.

10. Changes and contact

These terms may be updated as features and data practices change. A new version may update these terms and the privacy notice. Review the current text before using new features; the current prototype does not gate access behind a consent popup. For help, visit the Support page or Contact.
